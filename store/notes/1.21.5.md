An unofficial, community-built Decky Loader plugin that brings Nexus Mods
to Steam Deck and SteamOS Gaming Mode. Browse, install and manage mods
with a controller, without leaving the couch and without Desktop Mode.
Still in beta.

A **Nexus Mods Premium** account is required, because only Premium
accounts can start a download without a browser.

## Repair repairs

Reported in issue #36. Repair downloaded every mod with an installer
again, and on a large collection that meant 84 downloads to restore
nothing. It also never looked at ordinary mods, so a file that went
missing from one of those could not come back.

Repair now skips mods it can prove are complete, and restores any
installed mod whose files are missing, installer or not. The first Repair
after updating still checks each installer once; after that it skips the
ones that are fine. Checked on a Legion Go 2 by deleting a mod's main
file and letting Repair put it back.

## Switched-off mods say what they are waiting for

A mod left off because something it needs is off said "needs a mod that
breaks the game", without naming it. It now names that mod and gives its
reason.

## Collection sizes match My Mods

A collection page counted files and called them mods, so a collection of
460 mods with 566 files said 566 while My Mods said 460. It now says
"460 mods · 566 files".

Bannerlord, NieR:Automata, Palworld, Resident Evil 4, Skyrim Special

## Installing

Download `Nexus-Mods-1.21.5.zip` below and install it through Decky
Loader, or add the store URL from the README.
