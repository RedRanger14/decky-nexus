An unofficial, community-built Decky Loader plugin that brings Nexus Mods
to Steam Deck and SteamOS Gaming Mode. Browse, install and manage mods
with a controller, without leaving the couch and without Desktop Mode.
Still in beta.

A **Nexus Mods Premium** account is required, because only Premium
accounts can start a download without a browser.

## Mods with an installer no longer show as Failed

Reported in issues #35 and #36. When a mod with an installer (a FOMOD)
paused for its choices, the Downloads page marked it Failed straight away,
then ignored the success that followed a moment later when the collection
installed it with the curator's choices. The mod was installed; only the
Downloads page said otherwise.

Those mods now show Done once they install. A mod still waiting for your
choices says "Waiting for your choices", and a PC-only tool the plugin
leaves out says "Skipped", so "Failed" only appears for a real failure.
Checked on a Legion Go 2 by removing Blended Roads and letting Immersive &
Pure put it back.

## Problem reports keep the important part

"Report a problem" now puts recent install failures near the top. The
report is trimmed to fit a GitHub link, and that section was the part
being cut off.

## Supported games

Vegas, Helldivers 2, Mass Effect Legendary Edition, Mount & Blade II:
Bannerlord, NieR:Automata, Palworld, Resident Evil 4, Skyrim Special
Edition, Slay the Spire 2, STAR WARS Battlefront II (2017), Stardew
Valley, Starfield, Subnautica, Subnautica 2, Subnautica: Below Zero, The
Witcher 3, Valheim. Red Dead Redemption 2 is under construction.

## Installing

Download `Nexus-Mods-1.21.1.zip` below and install it through Decky
Loader, or add the store URL from the README.
