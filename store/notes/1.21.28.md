## Search finds every mod again

Reported in issue #43. The plugin remembers mods it has seen fail on your exact game version and keeps them off the store's front page. It was also hiding them from search, so a mod could vanish even when you searched for it by name. On the reporter's Cyberpunk 2077 that was Virtual Atelier and many other popular mods.

Search now finds every mod. The front page still leaves out mods that failed on your game version.

## Switching a mod back on clears its "broken" mark

If the plugin marked a mod as broken and you switched it back on, the mark stayed until the next game update, even through a reset, so a mod you were happily using stayed hidden from the store. Switching a mod back on now clears the mark. If the mod really is broken, the plugin will notice again the next time you play.

## Problem reports say what kind of trouble

"Report a problem" now says how many marked mods are broken and how many just need an update, rather than one number for both.
