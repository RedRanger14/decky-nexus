An unofficial, community-built Decky Loader plugin that brings Nexus Mods
to Steam Deck and SteamOS Gaming Mode. Browse, install and manage mods
with a controller, without leaving the couch and without Desktop Mode.
Still in beta.

A **Nexus Mods Premium** account is required, because only Premium
accounts can start a download without a browser.

## Fallout 4 DLC is recognised

Reported in issue #38: with every DLC installed, the plugin still said
mods needed DLC. There were three causes:

- Nexus names one expansion "Nuka World" while the plugin expected
  "Nuka-World", so it never matched.
- The free Creation Club content from the next-gen update (Enclave
  Remnants, Makeshift Weapon Pack, Halloween Workshop Pack) was not known
  to the plugin, so it always looked missing.
- The mod page said "Needs the DLC" without checking what was installed.

The mod page now shows nothing when every DLC a mod needs is installed,
and says a DLC isn't installed only when its files are really absent.
Checked on a Legion Go 2 against a full Fallout 4 install: the Unofficial
Fallout 4 Patch's nine DLC requirements all show as installed.

## Red Dead Redemption 2: a crash in Ultimate RDR 2 - Essentials

Installed beside RDR 2: Fixed and Enhanced, the Essentials collection's
WhyEm's DLC crashed Story Mode with "Unknown error FFFFFFFF". Found by
switching mods off one at a time on a Legion Go 2. It now installs
switched off, with the reason shown in My Mods, and everything else in
both collections reaches Story Mode. Switching it back on is one tap.

Bannerlord, NieR:Automata, Palworld, Resident Evil 4, Skyrim Special

## Installing

Download `Nexus-Mods-1.21.4.zip` below and install it through Decky
Loader, or add the store URL from the README.
